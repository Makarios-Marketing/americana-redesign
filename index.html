What We Do Most

Rust removal, welding and restoration.

Most companies look at corroded iron and quote a replacement. We look at the same railing and tell you which sections are still sound. If the iron can be saved, saving it is almost always the cheaper answer, and the honest one.

01  /  Strip

Rust Removal & Priming

We take corroded iron back to clean metal and prime it the same day, so the surface never sits open to Chicago weather. This is the single most common job we run.

02  /  Repair

On-Site Welding

Cracked joints, broken pickets, sagging gates and failed fire escape connections get repaired in place, so your ironwork never leaves the property.

03  /  Finish

Refinishing & Painting

Proper preparation and industrial coatings on fences, porches, balconies and stairs, so the repair still looks right several winters from now.

Already been told it needs replacing?

Get a second opinion before you spend the money. We will tell you honestly when a repair will not hold, and quote the replacement if that is the right call.
