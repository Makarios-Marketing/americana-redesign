What We Do Most

Rust Removal, Repair & Restoration

Most of our work is not new installation. It is bringing existing ironwork back: grinding out rust, welding what has cracked, and refinishing so it holds through another round of Chicago winters. If the iron can be saved, saving it costs a fraction of tearing it out.

Book an Assessment

Rust Removal & Priming

We take corroded iron back to clean metal and prime it the same day, so the surface never sits open to the weather. This is the single most common job we run.

On-Site Welding & Repair

Cracked joints, broken pickets, sagging gates and failed fire escape connections get repaired in place, so your ironwork does not leave the property.

Refinishing & Metal Painting

Proper prep and industrial coatings on fences, porches, balconies and stairs, so the repair still looks right several winters from now.
